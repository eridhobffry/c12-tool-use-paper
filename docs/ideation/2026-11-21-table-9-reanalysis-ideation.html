Grounding Context

The body of paper/main.tex is still empty. The reanalysis lives in notes/handoff-2026-11-20.md and as one-sentence bullets in notes/outline.md. Numbers were checked against Toolformer arXiv 2302.04761v1 on 21 Nov 2026. The PDF is not in the repo.

On T-REx, k=3 to k=10 moves the share of examples with a call from 82.8% to 98.1% and overall accuracy from 52.9 to 53.5. Accuracy on calls falls from 58.0 to 54.0. Accuracy without a call falls from 29.0 to 22.5. The handoff reads the overall score as the weighted average of those two rates, and it derives about 29.8 without a call and about 32.4 with one on the 15.3% newly called slice: about +2.5, with a rounding band of about +1.7 to +3.4.

Discussion is budgeted at 1.0 page and also holds the comparison table, selective calling, and the fix/break point. The conclusion is 0.3 page. The outline’s inference bullet already says “about +2.5” and the band, and it already says the figure is a net. It states only one nesting clause. The comparison cell in the handoff files the figure under “Eridho’s interpretation,” next to the composition effect.

Topic Axes

1. State both nesting legs

The inference sentence states two clauses, and it says Toolformer confirms neither. Questions called at k=3 are still called at k=10, and that subset still scores 58.0. The 1.9% never called at k=10 also scored 22.5 at k=3. “Still called with the same outcome” is not enough, because the without-call residual uses the second clause and the with-call residual uses the score, which the definition of k does not supply. The conclusion names both clauses. It does not replace them with the outline’s shorter line, and it does not repeat the arithmetic.

3. Move “harder” onto the inference sentence

The composition sentence stops where the cells stop. Overall accuracy is the weighted average of accuracy-on-calls and accuracy-without-a-call, so accuracy-on-calls can fall from 58.0 to 54.0 while the overall score still rises. That drop does not show that calls got worse. The cause — the newly called questions are harder — waits for the inference sentence, because it talks about a set of questions, and that set exists only under the nesting assumption.

4. Print both levels, and name the residual

The inference sentence says the newly called slice is about 29.8 without a call and about 32.4 with one, so about +2.5, and rounding alone runs from about +1.7 to +3.4. It says what 29.8 is: the k=3 group with no call, after the never-called tail is removed. It is not 34.9, the tools-off row, and it is not 29.0, the whole k=3 no-call group. The figure stays labelled as Eridho’s inference and as a net that cannot separate fixes from breaks.

It does not enter the composition sentence and it does not get its own about +2.5. The notes give no WebQS accuracy-on-calls or accuracy-without-a-call, so the mix reading and the residual are T-REx only. If the page is already full, the clause is the first cut.

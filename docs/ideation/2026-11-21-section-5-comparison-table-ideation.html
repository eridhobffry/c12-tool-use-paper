Grounding Context

The paper is an ACL two-column seminar paper. The body may run to 4 pages. paper/main.tex loads booktabs. Document text is 11 pt. Captions are 10 pt and sit under the float. Critical distinctions in a table should not depend on color alone.

The cell text is fixed in notes/handoff-2026-11-20.md (the evidence table, lines 39–44). Demonstrated, Measured proxy, and Not measured are source-anchored. Discussion as a whole is budgeted at about one page, and it still has to carry the axis, composition, reanalysis, and selective-calling bullets.

I2. Full-width ledger, checked at about 6 cm

Column width 88% Low
Description
Use one table* with booktabs, 11 pt text, a narrow stub, and two p{} columns for the papers. Do not use resizebox or tabularx; neither is in the repo. Before locking the cell wording, check the whole float, caption included, against about 6 cm. Any handoff sentence that leaves a cell is set inside that same float, after the tabular. The caption states that the numbers are the source papers’ table, figure, and section numbers. Headers can be “ReAct” and “Toolformer”; “Yao et al., 2023” and “Schick et al., 2023” can move into that caption. If the stub “Eridho’s interpretation” stays unwrapped, stop trying to fit three columns into 7.7 cm.
Axis
Column width
Basis
direct: template/formatting.md:98–100 (7.7 cm, 24.7 cm, 0.6 cm gap), :121 (document text 11 pt), :122 and :200 (captions 10 pt), :186 (wide tables may cross both columns). direct: the handoff cells at lines 41–42 are sentences, not the short token pairs in the template’s single-column tabular{lc} demo. reasoned: 0.25 × 2 × 24.7 cm = 12.35 column-centimetres, so a full-width float that is a quarter of the page area is about 6.2 cm tall.

A 6 cm table* blocks both columns, so the prose cannot sit beside it. The 6 cm figure is area math from the quarter-page target. It is not printed in the handoff. Keeping every sentence inside that height will force I1 or I3.

I5. Move the pointers into one legend

Source pointers in cells 64% Low
Description
Strip only the parentheses from the sentences. Under the tabular, still inside the float, print one legend: Demonstrated / ReAct: Table 1; Demonstrated / Toolformer: Table 4 and Table 9; Measured proxy / ReAct: Fig. 2; Measured proxy / Toolformer: Tables 3–9 and §7 yield; Not measured / Toolformer: §7 cost. The interpretation cells gain no source number. The legend says “Benefit depends on the task” is a reading of the ReAct Demonstrated cell, and the +2.5 sentence is a reading of “Diminishing returns (Table 9).” “Switching methods by a rule helps.” stays listed as having no source number. “Best combination order depends on the task.” stays in its sentence and is not assigned Table 1.

I6. Two paper cards in a single column

Column width 52% Medium
Description
Put two tabulars in one single-column table, stacked the way the ACL template stacks two tabulars in its accent example. Each card is headed by the paper name and uses the four row labels. Every handoff sentence stays in its card. The facing column stays free for Discussion prose. This is the single-column alternative to I2. It is not the default.
Axis
Column width
Basis
direct: template/formatting.md:98 fixes a column at 7.7 cm, and full-width tables are the exception at line 90. direct: the template’s accent table is one table holding two tabular blocks. direct: notes/outline.md:54 asks for one column each for ReAct and Toolformer in a single comparison table, which this shape does not do.
Rationale
If a full-width band would crowd the one-page Discussion, a tall single-column float can spend the same quarter page of area (about 12 cm of one column) and leave the other column for the bullets. The sentences fit a 7.7 cm card better than they fit a third of that width.
Downsides
The reader cannot compare FEVER against ASDiv by looking across a row. The outline’s comparison table is that across-row grid. Each card will still wrap, and two copies of the row labels spend vertical space. Choose this only if side-by-side scanning matters less than keeping the other column free.
